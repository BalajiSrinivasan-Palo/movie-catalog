**Epic:** E06 Quality, delivery and documentation  
**Priority:** Must · **Size:** M · **Order:** 32 · **Wave:** 13

**BRD acceptance criteria owned:** none

## Objective

Responsive and accessibility verification.

## Acceptance criteria

- `S-602.AC1`: Playwright viewport tests at 360, 768, 1280 px for every route: no horizontal scroll, touch targets ≥ 44 px.
- `S-602.AC2`: axe-core reports no serious or critical violations on every route; manual keyboard pass recorded in `reports/a11y.md`.
- `S-602.AC3`: Contrast 4.5:1 verified for tokens.
- `S-602.AC4`: axe also runs with a dialog open and on error and empty states, not only on page loads.

## Business rules and edge cases

- See the BRD rules and edge cases referenced by the acceptance criteria.

## Test expectations

- Playwright viewport and axe suites.

## Definition of done

Apply `docs/backlog/definition-of-done.md` in full, plus the criteria above. No criterion is complete without command output as evidence.

## Notes

Defects found are fixed in the owning component, with a regression test.
