# S-302: Thumbnails and artwork download in movie-service

**Epic:** E03 Artwork  
**Priority:** Must · **Size:** M · **Order:** 15 · **Wave:** 5  
**Status:** Draft (Phase 4)  
**Depends on:** S-301  
**Requirements:** FR-ART-05, NFR-PERF-05  
**BRD acceptance criteria owned:** none

## Objective

Thumbnails and artwork download in movie-service.

## Acceptance criteria

- `S-302.AC1`: Thumbnail (320 px wide JPEG, alpha flattened) generated at upload; failure to thumbnail rejects the upload as `artwork.corrupt`.
- `S-302.AC2`: `DownloadArtwork` server-streaming returns header then chunks for ORIGINAL or THUMBNAIL; missing → NOT_FOUND.
- `S-302.AC3`: Thumbnail of a typical 2000×3000 poster is under 60 KB.
- `S-302.AC4`: The pipeline from S-301 is extended, not duplicated: S-302 makes `thumbnail_path` mandatory for new uploads and cleanup removes both files.

## Business rules and edge cases

- See the BRD rules and edge cases referenced by the acceptance criteria.

## Test expectations

- Unit: scaling and aspect ratio, alpha flatten, tiny images are not upscaled.
- Integration: streaming download equals stored bytes.
