## Objective

Seed data script.

## Acceptance criteria

- `AC-FR-EXT-01-1`: as defined in `docs/requirements/BRD.md` section 10.
- `S-601.AC1`: `scripts/seed.sh` loads the real films in `scripts/seed/films.mjs` (ten public-domain classics plus Speed and Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone), the people credited on them, their credits and their posters through the GraphQL API; running it twice creates no duplicates. (Amended by D24 and D25; was about 30 invented movies and 40 people.)
- `S-601.AC2`: Every committed poster is in the public domain as marked on Wikimedia Commons, and its source is recorded in `scripts/seed/posters/CREDITS.md`; no copyrighted artwork is committed. Films marked `localPoster` get their poster only from the git-ignored `data/local-posters/` when present, and keep the placeholder otherwise. (Amended by D24 and D25; posters were generated.)
- `S-601.AC3`: Implemented as `scripts/seed/seed.mjs` (Node 22, built-in `fetch`/`FormData`, no extra dependencies) run with `pnpm seed`; film data is in `scripts/seed/films.mjs`; `scripts/seed/posters.mjs` reads the JPEGs in `scripts/seed/posters/`.
- `S-601.AC4`: Idempotency: movies by (title, year) duplicates are skipped; people by exact name match via `people(query:)`; artwork uploaded only when the movie has none; credits skipped on `credit.duplicate`.
