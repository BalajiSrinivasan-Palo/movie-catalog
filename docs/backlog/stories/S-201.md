## Objective

Create movie.

## Acceptance criteria

- `AC-FR-MOV-03-2`: as defined in `docs/requirements/BRD.md` section 10.
- `AC-FR-MOV-03-3`: as defined in `docs/requirements/BRD.md` section 10.
- `S-201.AC1`: `MovieService.CreateMovie`, Liquibase changesets (`movie`, `movie_genre`), gateway `createMovie`, `/movies/new` form (artwork picker added in S-304).
- `S-201.AC2`: Unique (title_key, year) violations from concurrent requests map to `movie.duplicate` (race-safe via DB constraint).
- `S-201.AC3`: `AC-FR-MOV-03-1` (movie appears in list and detail) is verified in S-203, which owns it; here the created movie is verified through `movie(id)`.
- `S-201.AC4`: On success the UI shows a toast and navigates to the new movie's detail page.

## Business rules and edge cases

- BR-01: a movie exists without artwork or credits.
- BRD s7 movie rules; genres from the fixed list, max 5, no duplicates; year 1888 to current year + 5 inclusive.
- title_key uses Unicode-aware lower-casing with `Locale.ROOT`; runtimeMinutes boundaries 1 and 1000 are tested.

- Integration: constraint race test.
